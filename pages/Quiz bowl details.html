Quiz Bowl

The deep dread of... collaborating in a group.

Nothing marinates the soul better than the cold sweat produced by forced collaboration. Teams will face off in rounds of mahjong trivia, challenges and cursed riddles from Count Doracula, every member of the winning team earns points toward the final score of the event.

How it works

  • Anyone can participate.
  • The teams are randomly generated on the night of the event.
  • All questions will one way or another be related to mahjong or Halloween.
  • Each team has a buzzer. First team to buzz in gets to answer.
  • The team with the most correct answers earns 10,000 points each.
  • The team with the second most correct answers earns 5,000 points each.
  • The team with the third most correct answers earns 2,500 points each.
  • Every participant earns at least 1,000 points no matter what.

Details still being finalized — check back closer to the event, or ask count.doracula@gmail.com.
