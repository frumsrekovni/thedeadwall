Space Mahjong

Doracula's cursed ruleset for 2026 — tiles be wrappin

In the spirit of Halloween and fear of the unknown, this year's games are played with Space Mahjong — a variant where the numbers wrap around, and the honor tiles can form sequences of their own. Surprisingly fun to get thrown into space and sucked into the void together.

Please take a quick look at Crow77 explaining it in this short clip.

In short: Space Mahjong is just like normal riichi mahjong with only three new key rules:

1. 1–9 number scale wraps around (so 8-9-1 and 9-1-2 are valid sequences that you also can chi)

2. The honor tiles can form sequences of their own (e.g., green-red-white of dragons is a real sequence).

3. You can chi from anyone.

The rules

Games are played with modified WRC 2025 rules. The modifications are:

  • Space Mahjong wrap-around is in effect: terminals connect (…8-9-1-2…) and honor tiles can form sequences (E-S-W).

    For example:
    has the wait 9m and 3m.

    has the wait East and North.
  • You can chi from anyone. In the case of multiple simultaneous chi, first in seating order takes precedence.
  • Open kokushi (Thirteen Orphans) is worth 3 han. Can be combined with other yaku.
  • Kokushi (Thirteen Orphans) can be won by robbing a concealed kan. Even if open kokushi.
  • Three red fives are used (1 for each suit).
  • No head-bump. (Multiple ron is allowed.)
  • Double yakuman for: Single-wait Suuankou, Thirteen-wait Kokushi, True Nine Gates, and Four Big Winds.
  • Uma is +30 / +10 / −10 / −30, split on a tie.
  • Time limit is 80 minutes. When time ends, players finish only their current hand.
  • Live audience is allowed.
  • 1-han special yaku — Bloody Mary: a hand entirely of manzu (characters) and Red dragon. Red dragon is not required. — courtesy of Anna
Mostly play online? Things to watch for →
  • Each player starts with 30,000 points.
  • No abortive draw (途中流局): a hand only ends when someone wins or the wall runs out.
  • The game only ends past South 4 or at the time limit.
  • No bankruptcy: play continues with negative scores.
  • Mangan rounding up: 4 han 30 fu and 3 han 60 fu score mangan.
  • Kandora is flipped before the discard.
  • Riichi may be declared with fewer than four tiles left in the live wall.
  • Liability payment applies for Big Dragons, Big Winds and Four Quads.
  • A double-wind pair is worth 2 fu.
