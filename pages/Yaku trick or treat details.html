Yaku trick-or-treating

Collect yaku as you play — will you risk it for the biscuit?

Yaku trick-or-treating is an event that runs across all of the Space Mahjong games — there's no sign-up, if you're playing you're already in. Every hand you win, you count the yaku you achieved on your trick-or-treat card (and, of course, into Count Doracula's soul ledger) and exchange it for treats.

How it works

  • When you arrive at the event you will be handed a trick-or-treat card.
  • Runs throughout all three Space Mahjong games — everyone playing takes part automatically.
  • Each achieved yaku gets marked on your trick-or-treat card.
  • Different yaku earn different treats — see below.
