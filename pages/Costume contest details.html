Costume Contest

Show off and flex your soul.

The costume contest is your chance to show off and flex your soul. Show up in costume — any costume! — walk a short runway, and that's it. Voting is then done anonymously by the event attendees, with criteria entirely of your own making.

It's not mandatory. Every participant receives points, and the winner gets a prize plus 10,000 points counted toward their final score for the event.

Costume contest rules

  • Everyone at the event can vote.
  • Anyone at the event can enter the contest.
  • You cannot vote for yourself.
  • You may vote for up to 3 different contestants.
  • You may cast at most 1 vote on each contestant.
  • The vote is anonymous to everyone except the organizer, who confirms it's readable and that you didn't vote for yourself.
  • The winner is the contestant with the most votes.
  • Tie for first place: a second round of voting is held between the tied contestants only.
  • Tie for any other place: all tied contestants receive full points.
  • Points are halved each place down, stopping at 1,000 points.
  • The voting criteria are entirely arbitrary — each voter decides their own reasoning.
